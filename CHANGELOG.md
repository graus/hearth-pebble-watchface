# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-22)

- **Flame height can follow the battery.** A new option under Flame height >
  Set by: a full battery is a roaring fire (height 10) that burns down with
  the charge to height 3 on an empty battery, the same readability floor as
  the outside temperature's, and builds back up on the charger. The watch
  reads its own battery, so it needs no phone or network, and the face only
  wakes when the charge or the charger changes. Stored settings carry over
  unchanged.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-07)

First release: the Doom fire in your digits on every Pebble.

- Flame height set by you, the outside temperature or the time of day.
- Wind set by you or the local wind, and flames that stay upright as you
  tilt the watch (color watches).
- Touch or flick to stoke the fire.
- Six color themes, including your own.
- Animate always, or only after a tap.
- A synthesised crackle on Pebble Time 2 and Pebble 2 Duo.
- An optional flickering backlight on Pebble Time 2.

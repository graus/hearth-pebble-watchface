The time in four large digits, each one a window onto a live fire. Hearth is inspired by Fireplace and draws its flames with the classic PSX Doom fire, computed live on your watch. The Doom fire is simple and efficient enough to respond to the world: the colder it is outside, the higher the flames; they lean with the local wind; they burn low by day and high at night, following your sunrise and sunset; and a tap or flick of the wrist stokes them. Six color themes (including your own), three resolutions, an "animate on tap" mode that saves the most battery, and on Pebble Time 2 an optional crackling sound and a backlight that flickers with the flames. Works on every Pebble, from the original to Round 2.

The time, burning.

Four big digits and a live fire. Pick your style: flames seen through the digits, or black digits standing in front of a fire that fills the screen.

The flames are the PSX Doom fire, the burning title screen of Doom on the PlayStation: about ten lines of code that turn a row of heat at the bottom of the screen into rising, flickering flames. It is so simple and cheap that your watch computes every frame of it live, with room to spare for a fire that reacts to you.

The fire responds to your world:
- The colder it is outside, the higher the flames.
- Low by day, high at night, following your own sunrise and sunset.
- A roaring fire on a full battery that burns down as it drains.
- Tap the screen or flick your wrist to stoke it.
- On color watches, the flames lean with the local wind and stay upright as you tilt your wrist, like a candle.

Also: six color themes (or make your own), a battery-saving mode that only animates after a tap, a crackling fire sound on Pebble Time 2 and Pebble 2 Duo, and a backlight that flickers with the flames on Pebble Time 2.

Works on every Pebble, from the original to Round 2. Inspired by Fireplace.
